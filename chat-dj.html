Live · Generative · Steerable

Talk to it. It plays.

Chat DJ is a live AI jam partner that learns your music folder and your genre. It writes and evolves music as code, in real time, while you steer it in plain language — and routes straight into your DJ rig so you can mix against it.

The signal chain

Code in. Sound out. Into your decks.

Chat DJ starts by learning your library — the tracks you own, the genre you actually play — so what it writes already sounds like your room. From there it does not render audio files. It writes live pattern code that the browser synthesises, then routes that audio into your DJ software as a deck: another player on the rig, not a playlist.

What else is in the box

A whole studio, on call.

Stems

Split any track

Separates vocals, drums, bass and everything else, so Chat DJ can solo, mute and filter each layer independently.

Key detection

Mixes in key

Reads the key of what is playing so generated patterns sit harmonically with the track on the other deck.

Visuals

Live visuals

Generative visuals driven by the same session state, so the projection moves with the music.

Your library

Plays your own records

Beyond learning from them, it slices your tracks live — chopped, looped and pitched straight into the pattern.

Modes

Autonomous or locked

Let it evolve on its own, or lock the pattern the moment it lands somewhere you want to stay.

History

Step back a move

Every pattern is kept, so a change that does not land can be reverted without breaking the set.
