02 · How we built it

A cricketing brain, we call the Context Engine.

At the heart of QRATOR sits our biggest bet — the Context Engine. A cricketing brain that watches every ball, weighs it against a decade of history, and decides in under two seconds which story is worth telling. That judgement, made hundreds of times a match across an entire IPL season, is what turns raw data into broadcast intelligence.

The Context Engine

If the generators are QRATOR's voice, the Context Engine is its mind. It continuously classifies every ball across twelve nested dimensions — phase, batter state, bowler state, tournament stakes, historical rarity, matchup type — and decides which stories should even be considered for firing, let alone published.

The engine's real output is not stories.
It is editorial judgement at scale.
